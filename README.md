# 📊 Marketing Campaign Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Marketing Campaign Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive data analytics project designed to evaluate the performance of marketing campaigns across different **campaign types, regions, and marketing channels**.

The dashboard provides a business-focused view of **revenue, marketing spend, ROI, campaign distribution, and campaign performance**, helping stakeholders understand where marketing investments are generating better returns.

The project demonstrates the use of **data analysis, KPI tracking, business intelligence, and interactive data visualization** to convert marketing data into actionable insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objectives of this analysis are to:

- Analyze total marketing **spend and revenue**
- Evaluate **Return on Investment (ROI)** across campaigns
- Compare marketing performance across different **regions**
- Identify high-performing and low-performing **campaign types**
- Understand the distribution of campaigns by type
- Provide interactive filtering for deeper analysis
- Support data-driven marketing investment decisions

---

The dashboard provides the following key performance indicators:

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| **Total Revenue** | 42.54M |

| **Best Performing Campaign** | Influencer Marketing |

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Revenue vs Spend by Region

The dashboard compares **total revenue and total marketing spend** across different geographical regions:

- Africa
- North America
- Asia
- Europe
- Oceania
- South America

This visualization helps identify regional differences in marketing investment and revenue generation.

---

### 2. Spend and ROI by Campaign

The dashboard compares marketing spend with average ROI across campaign types, including:

- Search Engine Ads
- Social Media Ads
- Content Marketing
- Influencer Marketing
- Radio Ads
- Email Marketing
- TV Commercials
- Billboards

The combination of a bar chart and ROI line makes it easier to compare **investment levels with campaign efficiency**.

---

### 3. Campaign Performance

The dashboard provides a comparison of campaign performance across different marketing channels.

**Influencer Marketing** has the highest displayed campaign performance value in the dashboard, while other campaign types can be compared using the same metric.

---

### 4. Campaign Distribution

A donut chart shows the distribution of campaigns between:

- Digital
- Traditional

This provides a high-level understanding of how marketing activities are distributed between digital and traditional channels.

---

### 5. ROI Overview

The dashboard includes an ROI overview visualization to provide a quick view of the overall ROI metric and its distribution within the selected data.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard using:

### Campaign Type
Filter the analysis based on specific campaign types.

### Region
Analyze marketing performance for individual geographical regions.

These filters allow users to move from an overall marketing view to a more detailed analysis of specific segments.

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard:

- The analyzed campaigns generated **42.54M in total revenue** against **25.69M in total spend**.
- The overall displayed **average ROI is 0.68**.
- **Influencer Marketing** shows the highest displayed campaign performance value among the campaign types.
- Marketing spend and revenue vary across geographical regions, indicating differences in regional campaign performance.
- Digital and traditional campaigns represent different portions of the overall campaign portfolio.
- Comparing **spend together with ROI** provides a more meaningful view of campaign efficiency than looking at revenue alone.

> These observations are descriptive findings from the dashboard and should be interpreted together with the underlying dataset and business context.
